# Chapter 3: Evaluate OKR outcomes

> **Read when**: Designing Friday wins, quarter reviews, spotting “all green = sandbagging.” Monday four-quadrant detail → ch04.

## Core Idea
Evaluation isn’t only quarter-end: **Friday win sessions** create weekly visible progress; quarter reviews recalibrate difficulty — **hitting every KR often means targets were too easy**.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Friday win session**: Show weekly outcomes (eng demos, design, deals, CS saves).
  - When: Distant goals, low energy, silos.
  - How: Fixed Friday; anyone can show; light celebration; “want something to share” drives the week.
- **Commit–celebrate weekly loop**: Monday plan/own; Friday celebrate.
- **Quarter-end calibration**: All KRs hit → likely sandbagging, raise difficulty; partial hard hits → healthy; all miss → recalibrate ability/plan (see ch04/ch05).

## Key Concepts
- Demos as alignment · sandbagging · P1 filter · multi-quarter milestones as compound focus.

## Mental Models
- Prefer **weekly visible wins** over quarter-end surprises.
- Prefer **raise difficulty** when the team feels good but isn’t growing.
- Treat celebration as **part of the execution system**.

## Anti-patterns
- Only looking at OKRs at quarter end.
- Celebrating 100% hit rate without asking if targets were soft.
- Empty Friday theater.
- Using celebration to hide strategic drift.

## Worked Example
Weekly demos and wins lift morale; a full KR hit rate draws a “are we sandbagging?” challenge; next quarter aims higher while keeping Friday wins — celebration and harder goals can coexist.

## Key Takeaways
1. Weekly visibility > quarter-end shock.
2. Friday wins build morale and cross-team respect.
3. All complete ≠ perfect; may mean too easy.
4. Focus includes saying no to non-P1 work.
5. Multi-year gains compound from weekly commit+celebrate.
