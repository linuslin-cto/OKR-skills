# Chapter 6: Final advice

> **Read when**: Decoupling OKRs from performance/raises, restoring “goal magic,” author checklists. If user mentions appraisal coupling → required.

## Core Idea
Restore goals as daily drivers of excellence — not paperwork for annual reviews. OKRs stretch the ceiling; raises/promotions run on continuous 1:1s — **never couple the scores**.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Appraisal-driven goals vs goal-driven performance**: Goals written for year-end become self-protection; CEO-only goals that never enter weekly time allocation also fail.
- **Five steps to restore goal magic**
  1. Goals define and drive success (clear short-term priorities; leaders overestimate shared understanding).
  2. Reject “most likely achievable” soft targets; OKRs seek the largest still-possible goal — **do not tie to appraisal**.
  3. Track in real time (weekly); monthly/quarterly-only checks find drift too late.
  4. Make goals findable in **~3 seconds** (mail/board); high performers start the day from goals.
  5. Top-down with bottom-up; hard control kills innovation.
- **How to assess performance**: ≥2× monthly 1:1s on engagement / performance / collaboration (e.g. 1–5 mutual scores); ~24 talks/year; year-end has no surprises.
- **Author checklist (ops summary)**
  - Usually one company OKR (except multi-line businesses)
  - ~Three-month challenge horizon; one-week wins are tasks
  - Os inspire — not classic appraisal KPIs
  - Weekly: company then dept; personal in 1:1s; confirm progress every week
  - Cascade top-down; OKR is the *must*, not the task dump
  - Monday is a conversation: confidence, health, priority shifts
  - Invite bottom-up input; publish OKRs (e.g. intranet)
  - Friday celebration buffers Monday intensity

## Key Concepts
- Goal “magic” / agency · toolboards for daily focus · Grove/Doerr lineage · adapt Google-scale practice to your size.

## Mental Models
- Prefer **goals driving the calendar** over **reviews driving goal docs**.
- Use **high-frequency 1:1s** to retire year-end surprise ratings.
- Treat **transparency** as focus infrastructure.

## Anti-patterns
- OKR scores → raises/promotions.
- Announce once, never repeat.
- Interrogating every personal OKR in the big weekly meeting.
- Stuffing every task into OKRs.
- Pure grind with no Friday buffer.

## Worked Example
Performance path: twice-monthly 1:1 dimension scores → fix issues same month → year-end is another known conversation. In parallel, OKRs stay public with weekly confidence — two systems, separate jobs.

## Key Takeaways
1. Decoupling is the precondition for stretch.
2. Goals must enter daily time allocation and 3-second visibility.
3. Weekly cadence + public + up/down inputs.
4. One company OKR, quarter length, conversational Monday, celebrating Friday.
5. Adapt to company size; keep the focus principle.
