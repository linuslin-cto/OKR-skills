# Glossary

**Commit–Celebrate** — Monday accountability + Friday small wins, with ongoing mid-week sync (Ch 3–4)

**Confidence index** — Subjective 1–10 odds of hitting a KR; often start at 5/10; ≤3 red, ≥7 green (Ch 4–5)

**Monday four-quadrant meeting** — This week’s tasks | four-week lookahead | OKR confidence | health metrics (Ch 4)

**Key Results** — Measurable proof the Objective is done; usually 3–4; hard but not impossible (Ch 1, 4)

**Tech qualification rate** — Example positive outcome metric for eng supporting sales (not task counts) (Ch 5)

**Sandbagging** — Deliberately low targets to guarantee “completion” (Ch 3, 5)

**Mission** — Long-lived, short, memorable direction; precedes OKRs (Ch 1, 4)

**Objective** — Qualitative, inspiring, time-bound, team-owned direction (Ch 1, 4)

**P1** — Highest-priority work; non-P1 can stop even if “pretty” (Ch 2–3)

**Friday win session** — Ritual where functions show visible weekly outcomes (Ch 3–4, 6)

**Health metrics** — Customer / team / system signals that can sink OKRs (Ch 4)

**Cascade** — Company → dept → optional personal OKR alignment (Ch 4–6)

**Hypothesis MVP OKR** — Kanban card with hypothesis + falsifiable KRs (Ch 5)

**Five-section OKR weekly report** — OKR+confidence → last week → ≤3 next deliverables → risks → notes (Ch 5; examples)

**Not-doing list** — Explicit drops this quarter to protect a single company O (SKILL / examples)

**Hard-fail checklist** — Too many Os | too-short cycle | O=appraisal KPI | no confidence | Monday theater | set-and-forget (cheatsheet)
