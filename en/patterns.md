# Patterns

## Single company OKR
**When to use**: Startup or single line of business; everyone must memorize the goal.  
**How**: One inspiring O + ~3 KRs; split only for truly separate markets/businesses.  
**Trade-offs**: Extreme focus; say no to secondary opportunities.

## 5/10 confidence writing
**When to use**: Setting or weekly-updating KRs.  
**How**: Start 5/10; discuss moves weekly; ≤3 red/help; ≥7 watch for sandbagging.  
**Trade-offs**: Subjective; needs psychological safety for honest numbers.

## Monday four-quadrant conversation
**When to use**: OKRs exist but execution drifts.  
**How**: Only the four quadrants; ~1/4 time looking back, rest forward; ban laundry-list theater.  
**Trade-offs**: Needs prep; trust daily judgment.

## Friday win session
**When to use**: Distant goals, low morale, thick silo walls.  
**How**: Each function shows visible outcomes; light celebration; “what I want to show next Friday” drives P1s.  
**Trade-offs**: Timebox; avoid empty ritual.

## Coached department KRs
**When to use**: Eng/design/CS struggle to quantify contribution.  
**How**: Disambiguate → align definitions → baseline → outcomes not tasks → positive framing.  
**Trade-offs**: Meeting cost; gains manageable alignment.

## Five-section OKR weekly report
**When to use**: Replacing “prove I’m busy” reports.  
**How**: OKR+confidence → last week → next 3 complete deliverables → risks → notes.  
**Trade-offs**: Transparency pressure; better coordination.

## MVP kanban card requires OKR
**When to use**: Feature debates with no priority basis.  
**How**: Before pull: hypothesis + KRs; rank by business contribution; review at due date.  
**Trade-offs**: Longer upfront talk; less rework/emotion.

## First rollout — pick one of three
**When to use**: Org fears failure.  
**How**: One company OKR; or independent pilot team; or project-level practice.  
**Trade-offs**: Narrower impact; avoids permanent abandonment after first miss.

## Decouple OKRs from performance
**When to use**: Coupling immediately causes sandbagging.  
**How**: OKRs stretch the ceiling; performance via frequent 1:1 dimension scores.  
**Trade-offs**: Two systems; required for real challenge.
